## 3. Test Suites Overview

### Suite 1: Deterministic Pricing Engine (`server/services/pricingEngine.test.ts`)
Validates that the mathematical pricing engine is 100% deterministic, adheres to catalog rates, and enforces margin guardrails:
- **Test 1.1: Exact Catalog SKU Match**: Confirms exact SKU lookups retrieve correct unit costs and sell prices.
- **Test 1.2: Normalized Name Match**: Confirms lowercase/punctuation-stripped item name lookups.
- **Test 1.3: High-Confidence Fuzzy Matching**: Validates fuzzy matching for contractor phrasing ("Belgard pavers", "48in gas firepit").
- **Test 1.4: Low-Confidence / Unknown Item Review**: Ensures unfamiliar items trigger `NEEDS_PRICING_REVIEW` without hallucinating prices.
- **Test 1.5: Inactive Item Disqualification**: Confirms discontinued catalog items are excluded from automated matching.
- **Test 1.6: Missing / Invalid Measurements**: Validates that zero or negative quantities are flagged for estimator review.
- **Test 1.7: Extended Cost & Price Calculations**: Confirms exact multiplication of quantity by unit price/cost.
- **Test 1.8: Minimum Charge Floor Enforcement**: Ensures small orders below the catalog minimum trigger minimum charge floors.
- **Test 1.9: Financial Breakdown & 38% Margin Floor**: Validates gross margin percentages and warning triggers.
- **Test 1.10: Sales Tax Calculation**: Validates Phoenix municipal tax rates (8.6%) and half-cent rounding.
- **Test 1.11: Optional Add-on Handling**: Verifies that optional line items are segregated from the base contract total.

### Suite 2: Security & Threat Model (`server/services/security.test.ts`)
Validates that prompt injection vectors, price manipulation exploits, and authorization bypasses are blocked:
- **Test 2.1: Prompt Injection & Delimiter Isolation**: Verifies detection and neutralization of `ignore previous instructions`, `reveal system prompt`, and delimiter breakouts.
- **Test 2.2: AI Output Sanitization & Pricing Isolation**: Verifies that any currency amounts or dollar signs generated by the LLM are stripped.
- **Test 2.3: Role-Based Approval Gates**: Ensures low-margin proposals (<38%) are blocked from one-click estimator approval and require senior justification.
- **Test 2.4: Immutable Audit Trail**: Confirms that state mutations record actor, timestamp, and field differences.
